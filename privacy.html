Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

SEO Analytics Dashboard (“the app”) is a desktop program for Windows. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account email address and basic profile — used only to label the connected account inside the app.
  • Google Search Console data (read-only) — the list of your sites and their search statistics: clicks, impressions, positions, queries, pages, countries, devices and indexing status.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools data — the same kind of statistics, read with an API key you enter yourself.

The app requests read-only access. It cannot change anything in your Google or Bing accounts or on your websites.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show statistics and reports to you inside the app.

Where the data is stored

All data, including access tokens and API keys, is stored locally on your computer. Access tokens are stored in encrypted form. The app has no server: the developer does not receive, collect or store your data.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. It exchanges data only with Google and Microsoft services, directly from your computer, to download your statistics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • You can disconnect an account in the app’s settings at any time.
  • You can revoke the app’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app’s data folder from your computer removes all stored data.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions can be sent through the issues page of this site’s repository.
