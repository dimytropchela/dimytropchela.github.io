SEO Analytics Dashboard

A desktop app for Windows that shows search statistics for your own websites in one place.

What it does

  • Connects to your Google Search Console accounts with read-only access and to Bing Webmaster Tools.
  • Shows clicks, impressions, positions, search queries and pages for all your sites, by day, week, month and longer periods.
  • Keeps the history on your own computer, so it stays available after Google stops showing it.
  • Points out sites whose traffic is dropping.

How it uses your Google account

The app asks for read-only access to Google Search Console data and for your email address, which it uses only to label the connected account. It cannot change anything in your Google account or on your sites.

All data is stored locally on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
